UserFlo - geographic data
=========================

world.json
  Country outlines drawn as SVG paths by bin/build-geo-data.js from the "world-atlas" package (ISC licence, Mike Bostock), which carries
  Natural Earth outlines (public domain, https://www.naturalearthdata.com). The two-letter country codes were matched using Natural Earth's own
  ISO code columns.

../../src/Geo/country-data.php
  Country list with English names, continent and a representative point, generated by bin/build-geo-data.js from Natural Earth's
  ne_10m_admin_0_countries file (public domain), plus a short hand-written list of territories Natural Earth does not list on their own.
  No licence conditions apply.

Natural Earth: "All versions of Natural Earth raster + vector map data ... are in the public domain." (Tom Patterson and Nathaniel Vaughn Kelso, and contributors)
